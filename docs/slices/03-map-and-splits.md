# Slice 03 — map and selected-traveller expense splits

21 October 2026. Owner requests a map and Splitwise-style bills shared by selected travellers, including named guests who have not joined. Existing authentication, saved trips and spending remain intact. No cloud resources or paid tile account are created.

## Contract

Core owns the ledger. Add migration 003 without deleting records: trip-scoped expense participants (accepted members plus organiser-created guests), stored expense shares and repayments. Member participant IDs reuse their account UUID; guest IDs are generated UUIDs. Guest records grant no authentication or membership rights. Archiving a guest/removing a member blocks new assignments and retains historical money. Old expenses are explicitly `none`/not allocated; never guess historical beneficiaries.

Expense POST/PATCH retains existing fields and `paidByUserId` compatibility, adds `paidByParticipantId` (mutually exclusive with paidByUserId), `split={mode:'equal',participantIds:uuid[]}` or `{mode:'custom',shares:[{participantId,amountPaise}]}` or `{mode:'none'}`. Required optimistic version on PATCH stays. Equal allocations sort IDs and distribute remainder paise deterministically; custom shares must be unique and sum exactly. Payer may be excluded. At least one selected person for a split, at most fifty; never infer the whole group from headcount. Persist shares atomically with the expense and append their snapshot to history. Existing requests without split remain recorded-only for compatibility. Changing an allocated amount requires an explicit revised split.

GET `/v1/trips/:id/expense-participants` lists active and historical participants. POST accepts `{displayName}` plus optional UUID retry key, organiser-only; DELETE `/:participantId` archives guests only. GET `/v1/trips/:id/ledger` returns participants, per-person paid/shares/repayment totals, zero-sum net balances, advisory transfers, unallocated spending, and recorded repayments. POST `/repayments` takes `{fromParticipantId,toParticipantId,amountPaise,date,note}` plus retry key; organiser or a member recording their own outgoing payment. No payment is executed. Check positive exact paise, valid trip participants and outstanding balances. DELETE `/repayments/:repaymentId` permits organiser/creator and retains history. Repayments never increase actual spending. After repayments, expense edits/deletion require organiser authority and explicit `acknowledgeSettlementChange`; existing repayments remain. All endpoints use existing membership/removed/outsider/deleted-trip boundaries and no-store BFF.

Website: preserve Budget summary; add selected-person equal/custom editor, per-person saved shares, guest management, balances, advisory settle-up and manual repayment recording. Local approximations of draft shares are previews only; saved shares and balances come from core. Show recorded-only legacy bills separately from split balances. Tests cover ₹4,000 split four ways in a larger group, excluded payer, one-paise rounding, invalid/custom totals, guest isolation, historical removed participants, retry, audit, repayment and correction.

Map: installed Leaflet 1.9.4, lazy client-only initialization, CSS imported through app layout, numbered local pin markup with safe text DOM nodes, matched teardown and ResizeObserver. Explore pins use exactly the filtered catalogue and open the same save action as list cards. Day map uses the selected day's coordinate-bearing activities in manual order; pins do not imply a calculated road route or travel duration. Text-only activities stay in the list. Keep keyboard/list actions and unavailable-tile state. Standard OSM tiles only for modest interactive local viewing, visible attribution, normal browser caching, no prefetch/offline/proxy; tile image referrer policy sends origin only (no private trip/invitation path). Production tile provider remains a separate account/cost gate.
